House Pi privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

House Pi is a private home project with a single user, the owner of the home it runs in. This page says what it reads, where that is kept, and who sees it.

What it reads

  • From the owner's Nest thermostat, through Google's Device Access program: indoor temperature, humidity, the thermostat mode and target temperatures, whether heating or cooling is running, and whether the thermostat is online.
  • The name of the home and the room the thermostat is in, as set in the Google Home app.

How it is used

The readings are used for one purpose: to show the owner the state of their own home and to alert the owner when it is too cold, too hot or too damp.

Where it is kept

Readings are stored on the owner's own Raspberry Pi, inside the home. They are not copied to any other server.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train any model.

This website

This site is two static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can remove House Pi's access at any time under "Third-party apps and services" in their Google Account, and can delete the stored readings from the Raspberry Pi.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the issues page of the GitHub repository that hosts this site.
