House Pi

A private home project. Not a product, and not open to the public.

House Pi runs on a single Raspberry Pi in one home. It reads the temperature, humidity and heating or cooling status from that home's own Nest thermostat, and warns the owner if the house gets too cold, too hot or too damp while nobody is there.

It has one user: the person who owns the home and the thermostat. Nobody else can sign in to it.

How it uses Google

House Pi uses Google's Device Access program to read the owner's thermostat. The owner grants that access with their own Google account and can remove it at any time in their Google Account settings.
